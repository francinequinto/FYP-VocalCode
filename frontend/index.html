VocalCode

How to Play with VocalCode!

1

Select or upload your sprites & background on the left.

2

Press Start Recording, speak clearly, then wait for the magic to happen!

3

Watch Llama 3 write the Python code that moves the canvas!

Here are some commands you can try:

📍 Positioning

"Move the chicken to the boy."

🔄 Multi-Spin

"Make the chicken spin 4 times".

🏃🏻‍♂️ Continuous Loops

"Make the boy walk all away to the right."

🔍 Scaling

"Make the tree 3 times bigger."

🐾 Group Selection

"Move all animals to the bottom right."

🗑️ Sprite Deletion

"Delete the chicken."

Tip: Clicking any example card above will automatically test that command!
